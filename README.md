## 📱 Our Featured Apps

### Screenshot Manager
A smart, lightweight utility tool designed to help users capture, organize, search, and manage device screenshots locally with extended cloud flexibility.

* **Local-First Processing:** Your media library and privacy are highly valued. All core image parsing, processing, and indexing occur directly on your physical hardware.
* **Optional Secure Backups:** Securely sync your organized screenshots to your personal cloud using our native, opt-in **Google Drive Integration**. 
* **Premium Tools & OCR:** Seamlessly access advanced options and extended processing credits via standard **Google Play In-App Purchases**.
* **Ad-Supported Tier:** We utilize **Google AdMob** to ensure core features remain entirely free to distribute and use for independent users.

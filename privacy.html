Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the mobile application software created by Ghori Productions (referred to as "the Developer", "we", "us", or "our"). The application is a utility tool designed to manage, organize, and back up screenshots locally and via integrated cloud solutions.

1. Information Collection and Use

Local Device Data & Media Permissions: To view, organize, and manage your screenshots, this application requires access to your device's media library (via READ_MEDIA_IMAGES or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE permissions). All primary processing occurs entirely locally on your device.

Optional Google Drive Integration & Backups: The application offers an optional feature allowing you to back up your screenshots to the cloud. If you explicitly choose to enable this feature:

  • The app will request authentication through Google Sign-In to connect to your personal Google Account.
  • The app utilizes the Google Drive API to create a dedicated folder and upload your selected screenshots directly into your own private Google Drive storage.
  • The Developer does not collect, store, or have access to your Google Account credentials, profile information, or any files stored within your Google Drive. Your cloud data remains entirely under your control.

2. In-App Purchases and Payment Processing

The application provides optional premium features accessible via In-App Purchases (IAP). All financial transactions, payments, and subscriptions are managed and processed exclusively by the Google Play Store Billing system.

  • The Developer never collects, handles, or stores any sensitive financial infrastructure data (such as credit card numbers, bank account details, or billing addresses).
  • Google Play provides us only with anonymous purchase tokens and transaction statuses to verify and unlock your premium features.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

To support development and provide extended features, this application integrates third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs). These services may collect device identifiers, performance metrics, and usage analytics in accordance with their independent privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: Used for serving advertisements. Collects advertising IDs, IP addresses, and diagnostic data to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads. See Google Privacy & Terms.
  • Google Play Services / Google Drive API: Used for account authentication, API structural synchronization, and backup routing.
  • Google Play Billing: Used securely to handle transactions and premium licenses.

4. Children's Privacy

This application does not knowingly address, target, or collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided third-party networks or integrations with information without your consent, please contact us.

5. Data Retention and Security

We do not maintain user registration databases or external cloud infrastructure servers of our own. Consequently, we do not store your personal identity profiles or media files. Third-party cloud processing (Google Drive) and billing processing (Google Play) are subject to the native cloud security measures implemented by Google.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published directly to this live page. You are encouraged to review this policy periodically for updates.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us at: ghoriproductions@gmail.com

8. Account and Data Deletion

Because our application utilizes Google Sign-In (OAuth) for optional Google Drive cloud backups, we do not maintain a remote database of your personal accounts or files on our own servers. Your data stays entirely under your control. If you wish to delete your application data and associations, follow these steps:

  • Local Data: To delete all local screenshots, data indexing, and ad caching data from your device, simply uninstall the application or clear the app's cache and data through your Android Device Settings.
  • Cloud Backups & Disassociation: To delete your backup data and completely revoke the app's access to your Google Account, navigate to your Google Account Third-Party Permissions Security Page, locate this application, and click Remove Access. This instantly and permanently severs the OAuth link and removes app access permissions.
